## Codex

**RECOMMENDATION: A—KEEP Court and add Remedy.** Estimated probability of an overall podium finish: **A 12%, B 2%, C 3%**. These are subjective forecasts, conditional on choosing each option.

**Reasons:** Court’s strongest differentiator is executable evidence controlling what Nemotron may claim. Remedy completes that mechanism: accuse → reproduce → rebut → repair → verify. This makes Nano/Super/Ultra serve distinct engineering purposes and directly fits this hackathon’s coding track and non-obvious-use criterion.

A fly-connectome scientist has greater initial surprise, but nine hours and $20 leave little room to establish simulation validity, meaningful experiments, and reliable execution. A notebook around model-generated experiments risks looking superficial to NVIDIA engineers. Prior hardware winners do not establish an advantage in this online competition.

**Top three must-dos:**
1. Deliver one authentic popular-repo case: existing suite passes, witness exposes regression, Nemotron repairs it, unchanged witness and existing suite pass on the repaired head.
2. Demonstrate epistemic restraint: one plausible accusation dismissed; show evidence, model roles, measured cost, and an equal-budget baseline.
3. Freeze features early; ship the hosted replay, reproducible commands, and a cinematic three-minute video centered on the repair.

**Risks:** Code review is crowded; courtroom decoration alone will not differentiate. Differential tests establish changed behavior, not correctness. Remedy scope can jeopardize submission polish.
