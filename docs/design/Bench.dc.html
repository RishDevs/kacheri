II. The cases before the court

  1. 32 planted bugs. Small edits, such as a flipped comparison, that the library’s 25 tests do not catch.
  2. 5 real regressions. Bugs the maintainers fixed upstream, re-introduced as innocent-looking refactors.
  3. 12 innocent changes. Renames and reorders that change nothing. Any conviction here is a false accusation.

III. Method

A conviction counts as correct when it names the planted bug’s file within three lines. The single reviewers read the same change and context, without having to prove anything. All results come from recorded runs and can be replayed.
